Habits are easier to keep when something small and alive depends on them. In Dayquest your habits, dailies and to-dos power a pixel hero. Finish things and the hero earns experience and gold, finds eggs and potions, hatches pets, furnishes a home and beats bosses. Miss your dailies and the hero gets hurt. Free, no ads, no premium, no account, works offline.

🗡️ YOUR DAY AS A QUEST
• Habits with + and −, dailies on chosen weekdays or every few days, to-dos with due dates and checklists
• Tasks shift from red to blue as you master them, and neglected ones pay more
• Kind mornings: before a new day starts, Dayquest asks which of yesterday's dailies you actually did
• Rest at the inn pauses damage for holidays and sick days
• Undo any tap, exactly
• Reminders per task and an evening nudge for dailies still open

⚔️ BOSSES
• Every finished task hits the boss, sometimes critically; every missed daily lets it hit back
• Twelve bosses, from the Slime King to the Hydra of Overcommitment, return as Elite, Ancient and Mythic
• A new boss of the week every Monday and a five-room dungeon every weekend
• At level 5 choose a Warrior, Mage, Rogue or Healer, each with three skills

🐾 88 PETS
• Pick an egg and a potion and see who hatches: 8 species in 11 colours, plus seasonal ones
• Name them; each hatches with a character of its own
• Ten hearts of friendship, tricks, morning notes, clothes and two evolved forms
• Saddle a grown pet and ride it through the scene

🏠 A LIVING PIXEL WORLD
• Your hero, pet and boss stand in a scene that follows the real time of day and the real season
• Ten places to unlock, from a sunny meadow to islands floating in the sky
• Rain, storms, fog, snow and a rainbow after the rain
• Gear you can see: try a helmet or a sword on before you buy it
• A room, an attic and a garden with over 60 pieces to place

🃏 SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• A card of the day twists the rules: a goblin steals gold, a merchant halves prices, a blood moon doubles damage
• Finish every daily for a perfect day: fireworks and a chest
• Daily visits climb a seven-day ladder of gifts

⏳ BUILT FOR YEARS
• An endless forge of gear tiers from level 20
• Real seasons with their own pets, gear and bosses
• 53 achievements, from a 7-day streak to five years together
• An album of big moments, collections, titles and statues

📊 ALSO
• A 12-week heatmap, streaks, statistics for every task and a yearly chronicle
• A journal with a mood a day
• Focus sessions of 15, 25 or 50 minutes with the hero on screen
• The tavern: swap one-line hero codes with friends, no server needed
• Relaxed, normal or heroic difficulty
• 8-bit sounds made on the phone, light and dark themes
• English and Russian

🔒 YOUR HERO STAYS WITH YOU
No account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Everything lives on your phone; move it to another one with a file or a one-line code.
